The Shekhawati Spice Collective

Spanning three desert-fringe villages in Rajasthan, this 340-family guild focuses on drought-hardy, traditional seed varieties. Heirloom Salem Turmeric and fiery Mathania Chillies are carefully harvested at peak morning dew and sun-dried on elevated, unbleached cotton charpais to prevent soil contamination.

Malwa Lakdi Ghani Guild

Preserving the ancient artisanal lineage of cold wood extraction, the Malwa Guild uses dense neem and babool wooden pestles operating at under 14 RPM. By ensuring pressing temperatures never exceed 38°C, native antioxidants, essential fatty acids, and the raw pungent aroma are captured without solvent chemicals or steam refining.

Integrity By Design

The Fair Partnership Pledge

Purity in the kitchen begins with dignity in the fields. We have structured our cooperative agreements around three immutable principles.

payments

Fair & Guaranteed Pricing

Smallholders shouldn't gamble on volatile middleman markets. We commit to contracts offering 25% to 35% above Mandi benchmark rates, disbursed directly to farmers' bank accounts prior to sowing season.

check_circle Zero Pre-harvest Debt Trap
eco

Chemical-Free Stewardship

Transitioning depleted soils to zero-chemical fertility requires support. We sponsor independent multi-stage soil and leaf microbial tests, distributing indigenous heirloom seeds and organic Jeevamrit culture freely.

check_circle NABL Accredited Testing
domain_add

Decentralized Heritage Units

Instead of centralizing processing in industrialized factories, we finance and construct village-level cold-press and slow stone-milling micro-units. The value-addition stays in the rural villages where women lead operations.

check_circle Rural Value Retention
